HOW IT ALL STARTED

Our Story

SkillNXT began with a simple frustration — too many talented students and working professionals were finishing courses full of theory but still felt lost the moment they had to apply it on the job. Weekday-only classes locked out full-time employees. Generic syllabuses ignored what recruiters actually asked for. We built SkillNXT to fix exactly that.

Today, that same idea drives every batch we run: live, hands-on projects instead of recorded theory, weekend-only schedules for Cloud Computing and Advance Excel so working professionals never have to choose between their job and their upskilling, and a placement cell that stays involved long after the syllabus ends.

The Problem We Saw

Bootcamps and degrees that taught theory but left students unprepared for real client work, real dashboards, and real interviews.

The Solution We Built

Weekend-flexible, project-first courses in Digital Marketing, Cloud Computing, and Advance Excel — designed with the syllabus recruiters actually expect.

The Impact We're Creating

5,000+ students trained, hundreds placed or promoted, and a growing community that keeps referring the next batch of learners.
