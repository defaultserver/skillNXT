GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courses, schedules, and training methodology.

Can I attend classes on weekends while working full-time?

+

Yes, absolutely! Our courses are specifically designed for working professionals and college students with dedicated weekend (Saturday & Sunday) and flexible evening batches.

Is the training completely practical and project-based?

+

Yes. 80% of class time is dedicated to live project execution in real cloud consoles (AWS/Azure), ad managers (Meta/Google), SQL databases, and data modeling tools. You build a portfolio of live work.

Can I take multiple courses like Python, SQL, and Tableau together?

+

Yes! Many students enroll in the complete Data Analytics bundle (Excel + SQL + Python + Tableau) for a complete end-to-end data stack. Speak with our academic counselor for bundle pricing and schedules.

What happens if I miss a scheduled class?

+

All live classes are recorded in HD and uploaded to your student portal within 24 hours. Additionally, you can attend backup doubt-solving sessions or sit in with another batch to review the topic.

Will I receive a recognized certification and placement guidance?

+

Yes. Upon successfully completing the course modules and capstone projects, you receive an industry-recognized SkillNXT Master Certification along with resume reviews, mock interviews, and placement support.
