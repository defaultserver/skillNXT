11 OFFICIAL MODULES

Complete NISM Series 5A Curriculum

Exhaustive coverage of regulatory standards, fund analysis, scheme categories, investor operations, and code of conduct.

Module 1: Introduction to Mutual Funds

₹2,000
  • Concept, operational mechanism, and structure of Mutual Funds
  • History and phased growth of the mutual fund industry in India
  • Key advantages (diversification, professional management) & limitations
  • Role of mutual funds in Indian capital markets & household savings

Module 2: Fund Structure & Key Constituents

₹2,000
  • Three-tier structure: Sponsor, Board of Trustees, Asset Management Company (AMC)
  • Role and statutory duties of the Custodian
  • Registrar and Transfer Agents (RTAs - CAMS, KFintech), Auditors & Brokers
  • Rights, safeguards, and statutory obligations of retail and institutional investors

Module 3: Legal & Regulatory Framework

₹2,000
  • Statutory role of SEBI in regulating mutual fund operations and investor protection
  • Key provisions of SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996
  • Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) - Role, objectives, and code of ethics
  • Know Your Customer (KYC), CKYC registry & Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA)

Module 4: Offer Documents & Disclosures

₹2,000
  • Scheme Information Document (SID) & Statement of Additional Information (SAI)
  • Key Information Memorandum (KIM) and mandatory investor disclosures
  • Monthly factsheets, portfolio disclosures, and risk-o-meter tracking
  • SEBI scheme categorization and rationalization mandates

Module 5: Fund Characteristics & Scheme Types

₹2,000
  • Open-ended, Close-ended, and Interval schemes comparison
  • Equity Schemes: Large Cap, Mid Cap, Small Cap, Multi Cap, Flexi Cap, Sectoral, Thematic
  • Debt Schemes: Overnight, Liquid, Ultra Short Duration, Gilt, Corporate Bond, Dynamic Bond
  • Hybrid Schemes: Balanced Advantage, Aggressive Hybrid, Multi Asset, Conservative Hybrid
  • Solution-Oriented, Index Funds, Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) & Fund of Funds (FoF)

Module 6: Metrics & Net Asset Value (NAV)

₹2,000
  • Computation and accounting formula of Net Asset Value (NAV)
  • Valuation guidelines for equity and debt securities
  • Mark-to-Market (MTM) valuation principles and amortized cost accounting
  • Total Expense Ratio (TER) regulatory caps & Exit Load structure impact

Module 7: Taxation & Applicable Laws

₹2,000
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT) and Stamp Duty applicability
  • Capital Gains Tax: Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) vs Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG)
  • Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) taxability & TDS provisions
  • Tax treatment for Resident Individuals vs Non-Resident Indian (NRI) investors

Module 8: Investor Services & Transaction Flow

₹2,000
  • Fresh Purchases, Additional Purchases, Redemptions, and Switches
  • Systematic plans: SIP, STP, SWP, and Top-up SIP mechanisms
  • Investment channels: Physical, MFU, BSE StAR MF, and NSE NMF II platforms
  • Cut-off timings and applicable NAV rules for liquid vs non-liquid schemes

Module 9: Risk, Return & Performance Measurement

₹2,000
  • Understanding risk types: Equity risk, Credit risk, Interest rate risk, Liquidity risk
  • Benchmark indices (TRI - Total Return Index) and benchmark selection rules
  • Return calculations: Simple Absolute Return, CAGR, and XIRR for SIPs
  • Risk-adjusted ratios: Sharpe Ratio, Treynor Ratio, Alpha, Beta, Standard Deviation

Module 10: Scheme Selection & Financial Planning

₹2,000
  • Financial goal mapping (Retirement, Child Education, Wealth Creation)
  • Asset Allocation strategies: Strategic vs Tactical Asset Allocation
  • Portfolio rebalancing rules and disciplined asset weighting
  • Evaluating scheme suitability based on client risk profiles and time horizons

Module 11: Ethics, Compliance & Code of Conduct

₹2,000
  • AMFI Code of Conduct for intermediaries and distributors
  • Commission structures: Understanding full Trail Commission model
  • Prohibition of upfront commissions and mis-selling practices
  • Due diligence requirements, Know Your Distributor (KYD) process
  • Best execution practices & conflict of interest disclosures
  • Redressal of investor grievances via SEBI SCORES platform & SMART ODR
